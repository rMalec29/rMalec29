## 🎮 Games

### [Beat Barrage](https://github.com/rMalec29/BeatBarrage)
An idle tower-defence game that sits in a corner of your screen, on top of your other windows, and **plays your music**. It captures your computer's audio, splits it into bass, mids and highs with a real-time FFT, and fires missiles, lasers and lightning on the beat. It has 20 charms, roguelike perks, prestige, a Steam leaderboard, and ships as a single Windows `.exe`.

`Python` `pygame` `NumPy` `Steamworks` `PyInstaller`

<img src="https://github.com/rMalec29/BeatBarrage/raw/main/gameplay.gif" width="420" alt="Beat Barrage gameplay">

### [Milk a Cow](https://www.roblox.com/games/72972766882718/Milk-a-Cow) · Roblox
A co-op farming and survival game for 1–4 players. Milk cows and sell milk by day, survive anomalies at night, and find the three pieces of the Golden Cowbell to escape.

📈 **4,000+ visits · 71 favorites** · released Oct 2026 · `Luau` `Roblox Studio`

### [Cut a Cornfield](https://www.roblox.com/games/96668796708376/Cut-a-Cornfield) · Roblox
A co-op game for crews of up to 8: clear five giant cornfields, find rare corn, survive scarecrow nights and take on the Corn King. Players earn Kernels to unlock classes, pets and machines.

📈 **334 visits · 15 peak concurrent players · 8 favorites** · released Oct 2026 · `Luau` `Roblox Studio`
